## Why inactivity does not necessarily mean abandonment

The validation records contain projects with later commits after inactivity intervals, including bug fixes, compatibility work, algorithm changes, releases, and documentation or manuscript work. Thus an inactive interval can precede resumed activity. The records do not establish off-repository work or intent during the interval.
